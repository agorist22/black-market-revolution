## About this game (long description — paste-ready)

### Black Market Revolution

Neon Market never sleeps. The forms do.

You’re a neutral operator with a small stake and too many doors: legal fronts, peer trade, mutual-aid co-ops that don’t need a press release. Fiat softens. Crypto moves — and draws eyes. Property pays until someone stamps a form. Briefings stay clipped: objective, risk, payout, maybe one dry joke.

The State smiles and files you. The Counter-Economy shrugs and trades. You’re the one in the middle with a car and a ledger.

**Feel we’re chasing** (ingredients, not clones): GTA 2–style top-down freedom · Tropico-style property itch · Suzerain-style pressure under shifting rules.

**Tone:** dry, street-smart, wry. Satirical rebellion with strategy underneath — not grimdark, not slapstick, not a manifesto, not a crime tutorial.

### What’s on the table (design fantasy)

- Hustle loops that reward clever workarounds over lectures  
- Grey-market and mutual-aid fantasy set against absurd compliance theater  
- Fiat vs crypto tension as *story and systems flavor*  
- Reputation heat: get noticed, get clever, or stay boring and small  

### What’s *not* a shipping promise yet

This page sells the fantasy and the tone. Playable systems (districts, currencies, missions, named contacts, faction win states) land when the team marks them live — not before. Early development. No fabricated wishlists, scores, or press quotes.

### Coming soon / early access framing

**Coming Soon** until a playable public build exists. When we ship a first look, it will be scoped honestly (one loop that feels good in about fifteen minutes — not “the whole city”).
