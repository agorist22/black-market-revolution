## Positioning

**Black Market Revolution** is a top-down open-world action-adventure with light management and political pressure. You’re a neutral operator in Neon Market — taxes, badges, and melting fiat on one side; peer trade, crypto, and mutual aid on the other. Play the loop, not the lecture.

**Feel comps (tags only):** GTA 2 freedom · Tropico-style property itch · Suzerain-style pressure.

**Tone:** dry, street-smart, wry — satirical and strategic, not grimdark, not slapstick, not a manifesto.

## About this game (Steam-style, DRAFT)

**Black Market Revolution** drops you into Neon Market with a small stake and too many doors.

Legal fronts. Grey-market runs. Mutual-aid co-ops that don’t need a press release. Fiat that melts. Crypto that moves — and draws eyes. Property that pays until someone stamps a form. Missions that read like ops briefs: objective, risk, payout, one dry joke if you’re lucky.

The State smiles and files you. The Counter-Economy shrugs and trades. You’re the operator in the middle with a car and a ledger.

Not a crime tutorial. Not a recruitment pitch. A satirical sandbox about clever workarounds under absurd rules.

*Early development. Fantasy first; playable systems when the team marks them live.*
