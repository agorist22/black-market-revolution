## Pitch

A fun, modern **GTA2-like** open-world game centered on an **Austrian-economics / Agorist** simulation. Statists vs Agorists battle for the market. Player starts **neutral**, then supports Agorists, Statists, or plays both sides. Feel: GTA2 freedom + Tropico / black-market / Suzerain ideas **only when they improve this game**. Emergent, strategic, replayable — not a mindless crime sandbox.

## Systems the owner cares about

- Dual economy: fiat vs crypto; inflation, taxes, tax evasion, credit/debt, supply/demand, business income, faction influence
- Legal / grey / black / mutual-aid businesses; property ownership (safer legal vs riskier untaxed)
- Jobs/production: farmer, craftsman, bootlegger, counterfeiter, dealer, gunsmith, scavenger, gov/private/entrepreneur, black-market work
- Dynamic NPCs: Neutral, Agorists, Statists, Criminals, Homeless, immigrants (regular/illegal), police, politicians, assassins, traders, bodyguards, special Agorists
- Goods: food, luxury, alcohol, tobacco, drugs, guns, armor, 3D-printed weapons
- Missions: smuggling, deliveries, recruitment/conversion, business work, political / assassination-related
- Vehicles: steal/buy, enter/exit, drive for travel/escape/missions
- Combat, wanted, police, assassins, bodyguards; defensive play supported
- World events: hurricanes, wars, tax raids, propaganda, crime spikes, deportations, elections, economic shocks
- Politics: **three politicians**; each vote is **two options only** (no “vote no”)
- Example bills: UBI, food stamps, tariffs, mass deportation, police crackdowns, war, drug legalize/ban, borders, CCTV, relief, stimulus, “incarcerate Bitcoin Jesus”
- **Neither faction wins immediately** — meaningful minimum play time; fairer, slower progression
- HUD: faction/market influence as a **clear bar**, not obscure numbers
- Menus that work: New / Load / options / credits / tutorial / Exit; save/load; pause; inventory
- Controls called out: WASD, E vehicle, F use, I inventory, T tax-evasion toggle
- Fullscreen desired but **stability first** (prior black-screen / freeze)

## NAP / tone

- Chaotic, rebellious, satirical, strategic, politically/economically provocative — still fun
- Agorism via **systems**, not exposition dumps
- NAP: unprovoked aggression vs non-aggressors hurts reputation; defense vs police/assassins/raids does **not**

## Menu chrome

## Special Agorist NPCs

- Unlock via notable counter-economic accomplishments (satisfying / secret discovery OK)
- Roam map; visible to Agorist-aligned players; Statists cannot locate them
- High survivability: armor, 3D-printed rifles, ammo, enhanced health
- **Brandon Aragon:** grey-market farmer; tax-free food; spreads Agorism / converts NPCs
- **Sal Mayweather:** 3D-printed businessman; primarily **crypto** / counter-economy (not fiat)
- Both evade taxes, prefer crypto
- On death: notify player; remove that NPC’s bonuses
